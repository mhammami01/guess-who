Danger of LLMs!

Personal Data Security

Never share sensitive information: Avoid inputting personal data, passwords, or confidential information into LLMs.

Information Verification

Always verify important information: LLMs can make mistakes or "hallucinate" false information. Double-check critical facts from reliable sources.

Critical Thinking

Critical thinking is essential: LLMs can present incorrect information confidently. Always maintain a healthy skepticism and verify important claims.

Responsible Use

Use responsibly: Don't use LLMs to generate harmful content, spread misinformation, or to make you do all your homeworks.
