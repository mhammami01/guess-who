Welcome to Guess Who!

Are you ready to play to Guess Who with an AI?

This isn't your ordinary Guess Who - you'll be interacting with an AI language model to discover a mystery person! Get ready to learn about AI while having fun!

How the Game Works

  1. The class splits into two teams.
  2. Team 1 chooses a mystery person and sets up the AI with special instructions.
  3. Team 2 asks questions to the AI to figure out who the mystery person is.
  4. You can ask any yes/no questions about the person.
  5. Try to guess the person in as few questions as possible!

Understanding Your AI teammate

Your AI teammate is a Large Language Model (LLM)! It has read millions of books and articles. It can understand and respond to your questions.

It can't see or hear you, so clear writing is important. It's not powerful as our mind, so it can make mistakes

Tips for Great Questions

The key to winning is asking smart questions!

Be specific: "Did this person live in the 20th century?" works better than "Is that person old?"

One detail at a time: "Was this person a scientist?" is better than "Was this person a scientist who won awards and lived in Europe?"
